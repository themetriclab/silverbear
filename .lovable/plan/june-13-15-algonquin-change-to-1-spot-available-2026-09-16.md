## Doing it yourself without spending credits

Small text changes like availability, prices, and dates live in one friendly data file. Options:

1. **Edit on GitHub** (if your project is connected to GitHub): open the repository, find `src/data/tours.ts`, click the pencil icon, change the text in quotes (e.g. `"2 Spots Available"` to `"1 Spot Available"`), and commit. The site updates automatically.
2. **Lovable's free tools**: visual edits (selecting text on the page in edit mode) are cheaper than chat messages for simple wording changes — though data-driven text like availability is safest changed in the file.
3. **Batch your asks**: combine several small changes into one message so each credit covers more work.
